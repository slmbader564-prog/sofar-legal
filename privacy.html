Privacy Policy: Sofar

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Sofar keeps your life atlas on your phone. Your places never leave your device. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app works fully offline.

Information we collect

None. Sofar does not collect, store on servers, transmit, or share any personal data. We have no servers. Everything the app records lives only on your device.

Information stored on your device

To build your atlas, Sofar saves the following locally on your device only. It never leaves your phone and is not accessible to us or anyone else:

  • Places you log: coordinates, timestamps, names, and any notes or photos you attach.
  • Statistics, medals, and streaks computed from your own places.
  • Your preferences (theme, notifications, and similar settings).
  • Whether you have unlocked Sofar Complete.

You can export all of this data at any time from Settings, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Location

Sofar uses your location only while you are using the app, only at the moment you tap to log a place. There is no background location tracking, ever. If you enable the optional nearby memories feature, the app registers a small number of places with your phone so Android can notify you when you are near one; that processing happens on your device.

Photos

Photo access is optional. If you allow it, Sofar reads photo location tags on your device to help fill in your atlas. Photos are never uploaded anywhere.

Leaderboards (optional)

Sofar can connect to Google Play Games if you choose to sign in. This is off by default. If you turn it on, only your totals (number of countries, places, and streets) are submitted to leaderboards. Your actual places, coordinates, names, and dates are never shared. Google Play Games is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Sofar offers a single optional one-time purchase, Sofar Complete. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive, see, or store your payment details.

Notifications

All notifications (such as anniversaries of your own places) are generated on your device. Nothing is pushed from a server, because there is no server.

Children

Sofar is suitable for a general audience. Because the app sends no data off the device, it collects no data from children.

Third parties

Sofar contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The only third party involved is Google Play, which distributes the app, processes the optional purchase, and, only if you opt in, operates the leaderboards.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
